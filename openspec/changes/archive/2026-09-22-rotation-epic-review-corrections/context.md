# Decisions and limits

The user requested the next step after the review: correct the confirmed defects and requalify in isolation. This does not authorize commit/merge, production replacement or rotation activation. Source and effects stay default-OFF.

Reset: an outer worker check cannot protect the awaited central detail fetch/pin. The central executor receives an optional final admission hook. Rotation supplies an exact plan/member/intent and freshness guard at that boundary; ordinary callers retain their existing behavior. A pin already persisted before rejection remains durable and does not become permission to replay.

Recovery: the fixed single-evaluation plan is deliberately conservative. Interrupted pre-effect work is not automatically requalified because reset/effect certainty must not be reconstructed from display flags. The scheduler exposes durable needs_attention with a specific recovery reason, preserves snapshots, quota and binding, and performs no new reset/start. A future operational recovery requires separately reviewed evidence; deleting a binding or minting a new UUID is not recovery.

Example: if a controller retained outgoing final Usage and created a preview before interruption, the next worker invocation records attention instead of claiming to evaluate forever. Repeated invocations preserve the same ownership and do not spend another credit.

The deployed generation remains b98 with source manifest e55; the corrections in this change are not deployed merely because their OpenSpec is archived. Old sealed reports retain their original point-in-time claims. Current explanatory documents will link the newer OFF deployment and its read-only observer.
